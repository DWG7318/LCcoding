# Delivery Method Q&A

Delivery Method Q&A begins only after `POST_SECURITY_OWNER_ACCEPTED`.

Before asking questions, load:

- Owner Policy;
- Project Profile;
- customer contract/material;
- final security-closed candidate;
- Post-Security Owner Acceptance receipt;
- prior delivery decisions.

Ask only unresolved customer-specific questions, give a recommended answer and concise options, and persist every answer in `DELIVERY_DECISION`.

Confirm delivery model, included/excluded assets, source and modification rights, runtime/infrastructure, data migration and responsibility, internal dependency consumption, license scope, activation, upgrades, support, launch, rollback, credentials, and maintenance.

Owner-locked internal exclusions are constraints, not customer options.

# Workflow and UI Dual-End Design

Workflow and UI are equal product ends.

After the Simulation World foundation exists, Workflow and UI advance independently and may proceed concurrently. Each must become independently runnable and inspectable; plans, shells, or mocks do not qualify. Cross-layer connection is not an early Product Formation condition and remains for Feature Slice and UI-locked Integration. Product meaning and scenario identifiers still synchronize across both ends and Simulation.

Workflow defines capability, state, rules, authority, side effects, failure, and recovery.
UI defines every actor-facing entry, action, state, feedback, permission, and accepted visual result.

Use IDs and traces rather than copying the same actor, state, and rule definitions into multiple documents. A mismatch is recorded once and resolved across both ends.

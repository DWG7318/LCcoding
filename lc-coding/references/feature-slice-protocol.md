# Feature Slice Protocol

A Feature Slice is one actor-visible capability from UI entry through Integration and
Workflow to state, side effects, and visible result. The actor may be a customer, staff
member, operator, administrator, partner, or system-facing recipient such as a
notification or audit viewer.

It must define Calabash trace, Simulation scenarios, Integration Baseline,
Verification, Owner Acceptance, and affected shared capabilities. Technical layers alone
are not product progress.

# Verification and Owner Acceptance

AI Verification proves the frozen Feature contract with reproducible evidence. Owner
Acceptance judges whether the running product is the right product. Both are required for
product-facing work. Verification cannot waive an Integration Baseline violation.

Verification and acceptance must cover every in-scope actor surface, including staff and
administrator back-office UI, notification surfaces, audit views, and status surfaces.
Customer-facing success alone is not enough when the Workflow depends on internal
operation or control.

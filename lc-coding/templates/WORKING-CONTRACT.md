# Working Contract

## Owner authority

Product direction, Workflow intent, UI direction, material scope, Calabash amendments, version promotion, acceptance, delivery, cost.

## AI authority

Routine analysis, planning, tooling, integration, implementation, repair, verification, synchronization, and packaging inside frozen boundaries.

## Prohibited escalation

Do not ask Owner to inspect code/logs or approve ordinary technical fixes.

## Protected artifacts

Product Baseline, locked UI, accepted Feature Slices, Owner Policy, Project Profile, delivery exclusions.
